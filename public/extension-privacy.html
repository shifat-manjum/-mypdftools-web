Privacy Policy for MyPdfTools Capture

Last Updated: September 29, 2026 • Official Chrome Web Store Disclosure

Zero Data Collection Policy: MyPdfTools Capture operates 100% locally on your computer. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or captured screenshot content to any external server or third party.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

MyPdfTools Capture does not collect any user data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, email, address, or phone number.
  • No Web Browsing History: The extension does not track which websites you visit, your search queries, or your browser activity.
  • No Screenshot Content: All captured screenshots, images, redacting blur blocks, and PDF exports are created and processed entirely within your local browser memory (HTML5 Canvas). No image bytes are ever transmitted to any server.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The extension contains zero tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, telemetry, or remote advertising scripts.

2. Permissions and How They Are Used

MyPdfTools Capture requests the absolute minimum permissions required to perform screenshot capturing on your active tab:

Permission Type Plain-English Purpose
activeTab User-Invoked Access Grants temporary access to capture visible slices of the current webpage only when you explicitly click the extension icon or press Alt+Shift+P. It does not monitor inactive tabs.
scripting API Allows the extension to inject the auto-scrolling script to measure document height and scroll the active page to capture below-the-fold content.
storage Local API Used exclusively for local temporary handoff of screenshot slice metadata between the background service worker and the newly opened preview studio tab. No data leaves your machine.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension does not connect to or communicate with any third-party APIs, servers, or external services during capture or preview generation.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all screenshot processing happens locally in volatile browser memory, closing the preview studio tab automatically discards all in-memory image buffers. When you download a PNG or PDF file, the file is saved directly to your local computer's Downloads directory and remains under your exclusive control.

5. Compliance with Chrome Web Store Policies

MyPdfTools Capture adheres strictly to the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the Single Purpose Policy, Minimal Permissions Policy, and User Data Privacy Guidelines.
